Featured Play of the Day · Fundamental

Force Out vs. Tag Out

Two ways to get a runner out: step on the base with the ball, or tag the runner directly.

Quick rule: if a runner behind you is forcing you to run to the next base, the defense only needs to touch the bag. If nobody is pushing you forward, they have to tag you.
